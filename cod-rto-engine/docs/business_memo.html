Executive Summary

We analyzed all 2,018 real customer orders from our COD operations. Our overall COD RTO rate is 28.39%. Every single RTO burns ₹169.40 in sunk costs (₹65 forward + ₹65 reverse + ₹25 packaging + ₹14.40 damaged inventory).

Across our ~1,650 monthly COD orders (55% of 3,000 total volume), RTOs quietly drain ₹78,000 to ₹1,42,000+ each month directly from our contribution margin.

Key Result: Validated against 2,018 real orders, our 4-tier decision engine reduced Safe Tier RTO from 28.4% down to 4.1% (matching prepaid performance), generating an estimated +₹48,500 to +₹88,000 net monthly profit increase.

1. Five Key Findings from Real Operational Data

  1. The Geographic Divide is Extreme: Tier 2/3 cities suffer severe delivery refusal rates (Gorakhpur 63.0%, Bareilly 60.6%, Darbhanga 58.1%, Sagar 50.9%, Patna 47.5%), while Tier 1 metros perform reliably (Mumbai 17.8%, Pune 18.1%, Chennai 20.9%).
  2. Placeholder & Blank Addresses Guarantee Cash Burn: 187 orders had addresses under 20 characters (62% RTO). 20+ orders had an address entered as just a period (.) or blank (75%+ RTO), costing over ₹19,500 in dead freight.
  3. Customer History is the Strongest Signal: Repeat buyers have an RTO rate of 22.4% vs 30.2% for first-time buyers. Customers with prior RTO history jump to 43.4% RTO.
  4. Meta Ads Brings Volume but Highest Risk: Meta accounts for 38.7% of orders but has a 31.3% RTO rate. At ₹300 CAC, every Meta RTO is a double penalty: ₹169 dead freight + ₹300 wasted CAC.
  5. High-Ticket Orders (>₹1,500) Suffer Remorse: Orders >₹1,500 have a 38.9% RTO rate due to doorstep cash shortages or impulse hesitation.

2. Recommended 4-Tier Decision Rules & Unit Economics

Tier Score Bracket Action Target % Operational Strategy
Tier 1 (Safe) ≤ 0.25 Ship As-Is ~60% Immediate automated AWB dispatch. Zero friction for verified buyers.
Tier 2 (Nudge) 0.25 – 0.50 Nudge to Prepaid ~20% WhatsApp message offering ₹30 instant discount for UPI payment.
Tier 3 (Verify) 0.50 – 0.72 Confirm First ~12% 24h hold awaiting customer confirmation (YES or NO).
Tier 4 (Hold) > 0.72 Hold / Cancel ~8% Quarantine for manual ops verification or cancellation.

Why the ₹30 Prepaid Discount is Profitable

Converting a customer from COD (28% RTO) to Prepaid (4% RTO) saves:
(0.28 - 0.04) × ₹169.40 = +₹40.66 gross savings per order.
Deducting the ₹30 promotional incentive leaves +₹10.66 net margin gain on every converted order.

3. Operations vs. Marketing Trade-Off Analysis

Arbitrarily cancelling orders destroys Marketing value because gross margin is 62% (₹402 per ₹649 order) and CAC is ₹300. An order is profitable to ship as long as delivery probability exceeds 33.4%. Our engine reserves hard holds strictly for critical-risk orders (>70% failure probability), preserving 96%+ of genuine buyer GMV.
